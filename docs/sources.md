## Decisions by the owner (2026-11-10)

- Mathematics AA and AI: leave out the HL-only prior learning items.
- Physics prior skills: basic arithmetic and algebra; decimals, fractions and percentages; ratios and reciprocals; exponents; scientific notation; mean and range; rearranging equations; direct and inverse proportion; areas and volumes. Approximation and estimation and percentage change left out. Teachers choose 7 for the hexagon.
- Chemistry uses the same selection rules (decided by the curator, to be confirmed); rearranging equations added because the mole calculations need it.
- Economics: no prior learning in the guide, so the prior skills are those its calculations use; HL adds the HL-only calculations.
